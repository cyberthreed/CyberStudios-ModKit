## 🍷 a little peek behind the pixels

Mixed drinks can take your Zois through five stages, from Feelin’ Good to Blackout. The mod adds custom emotions, interactions, hangovers, and consequences for heavy drinking.

## ✧ inside the project

The [data patches](Content/JsonPatch) define gameplay and effects. [Lua scripts](lua) handle runtime behavior, and the [English localization](l10n/en_mod) and [string tables](Content/String) hold in-game text.

The [plugin descriptor](TipsyZois_899D9EC7.uplugin) and [project metadata](Meta) belong to the inZOI ModKit project. Use a compatible version of the official ModKit for development. For gameplay, use the official packaged download above.
